Role Risk Score

How RolegacyAI calculates the knowledge continuity risk for any role — and how organisations use it to prioritise where to invest in memory capture before it's too late.

The Risk of Waiting

Most organisations only think about knowledge continuity when someone announces they are leaving. By that point, the outgoing role holder is typically distracted, time is short, and the depth of capture that is achievable in a few weeks is a fraction of what could have been accumulated over months or years.

The Role Risk Score is designed to move this thinking earlier. It calculates the current knowledge continuity risk for every role in scope — so organisations can prioritise capture investment before a transition is imminent, not after it is announced.

Risk Factors

The Role Risk Score is a composite metric built from multiple risk signals:

  • Coverage score: Roles with low coverage — significant knowledge domains that are undocumented — carry higher risk. A role transition with low coverage means the successor inherits significant blind spots.
  • Confidence score distribution: A role with many low-confidence entries is more risky than one where the captured knowledge is well-validated, even if the entry count is similar.
  • Holder tenure: Long-tenured role holders carry more accumulated institutional knowledge. The longer the tenure, the larger the potential knowledge loss if the role is not well-documented before transition.
  • Memory age: Roles where memory entries have not been updated or reviewed recently carry higher risk — the institutional knowledge may have drifted from the documented state as the role evolved.
  • Transition indicators: Where available, signals that a transition may be approaching — such as reported succession planning, role restructuring, or organisational change — are factored into the risk calculation.
  • Role criticality: Roles with higher operational impact — more dependencies, larger budgets, wider stakeholder reach — receive a risk multiplier that reflects the greater consequence of knowledge loss.

Risk Bands and Alerts

The Role Risk Score is expressed as a numerical score and mapped to risk bands: Critical, High, Medium, and Low. Critical and High-risk roles trigger configurable alerts — to HR, to operations leadership, or to the role holder directly — recommending immediate capture investment. Organisations can configure alert thresholds, notification channels, and escalation paths based on their own governance requirements.

Executive and Portfolio View

Risk scores are aggregated into a portfolio view that gives HR, operations leadership, and executive sponsors visibility into the organisation's overall knowledge continuity risk position. This view surfaces where the greatest institutional knowledge vulnerabilities sit, enabling proactive investment in capture and documentation before the cost of transition failure is incurred.
