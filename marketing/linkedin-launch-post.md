# LinkedIn launch post

RolegacyAI is live — and we're opening support for the first independent report.

The report explores a simple but uncomfortable question:

If AI helps someone improve the efficiency, quality, or impact of their role, how should that value be measured, recognised, preserved, and shared?

Most organisations are moving fast on AI adoption.

But the human side is still under-defined:

- Who gets credit for AI-enabled role improvement?
- How should productivity uplift be measured?
- Can recognition models keep pace with automation?
- What position should organisations take before trust breaks down?
- Can AI adoption create shared value instead of silent extraction?

RolegacyAI is being built to explore this space through role-scoped memory, research, practical frameworks, and pilot-ready models.

We're looking for early readers, advisors, design partners, pilot organisations, and founding supporters.
