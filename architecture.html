Architecture

How RolegacyAI Works

RolegacyAI is built as a layered architecture — from the moment role knowledge is captured, through classification and sanitisation, to retrieval, scoring, and the generation of a successor brief. Each layer has a specific function. Together, they turn distributed, undocumented institutional knowledge into a structured, trustworthy, transferable role memory.
